PRIVACY POLICY

Your details.
Handled with clarity.

How this website handles the information you choose to share.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Enquiry information

The enquiry form asks for your name, phone number, project location, service and project description. You may also provide an email address, approximate project size and estimated budget. These details are used to prepare a message addressed to Fieldtech Construction.

How the form works

The form prepares a WhatsApp link in your browser. It does not submit your details to a website database or email service. When you open WhatsApp, your details are included in the prefilled message. You decide whether to send that message. WhatsApp processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Direct contact

If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, the information you share is received through that communication service and may be used to discuss your requirements and respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sharing sensitive information that is unnecessary for your construction discussion.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not store form details in your browser. Photographs and fonts are served with the website. The hosting provider may process technical logs needed to serve and secure the website. The private website preview may also require hosting-provider sign-in.

Questions about your information

Contact Honey Jaiswal at sales@fieldtechconstruction.com or +91 85305 65989 to ask about information you have shared or request correction or deletion.

Details pending confirmation

The company’s legal entity details, business address, enquiry retention period and any additional privacy obligations are pending confirmation. This policy should be reviewed when those details are available or if enquiry handling changes.
